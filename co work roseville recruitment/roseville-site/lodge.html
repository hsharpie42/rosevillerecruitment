About The Lodge

A quiet village home, on the outskirts of York.

The Lodge is set in our quiet village location in Heslington on the outskirts of the historic city of York — the perfect place for your loved one to be cared for. Heslington possesses great walking routes, a nearby duck pond and other local village amenities.

Here at The Lodge, our residents are at the forefront of everything we do. We have a vastly experienced, stable staff team, all of whom are always ready and willing to offer the very best care and support.
